Professional Profile

Researcher, statistician, data scientist, and academic with an interdisciplinary background in research methodology, quantitative analysis, academic writing, scientific communication, statistics, mathematics, and data science.

Michael holds a First Class BSc (Hons) in Statistics and an MSc in Mathematics (Data Science Option) from the Pan African University Institute for Basic Sciences, Technology and Innovation (PAUSTI), Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT), Kenya.

Research and analytical expertise

Experienced across the full research process: problem formulation, literature review, research objectives and questions, methodological design, data collection and management, statistical analysis, interpretation, academic reporting, and manuscript preparation.

Proficient in Python, R, MATLAB, SPSS, LaTeX, and Microsoft Office, with experience in statistical modelling, data preprocessing, predictive analytics, time-series analysis, model evaluation, data visualisation, and reproducible scientific computing.
